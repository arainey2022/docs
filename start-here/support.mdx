## Contact our team

If your plan includes live chat, use the **live chat icon in the bottom left corner** of your My AskAI dashboard. Use this route for support, bug reports and feedback. Our team can also help by email.

Scale includes hands-on help with helpdesk integrations and help to investigate or optimise conversations, guidance and Tasks. Enterprise includes the additional support listed above.

## Get help on Pro

Ask **Echo** for help in your dashboard. Echo can search the help docs for you. If you need live chat, email or setup calls with our team, upgrade to Scale.

If you are completely stuck, find a possible platform bug or want to suggest an improvement, ask Echo to pass a report to our team. Include a screenshot where possible. Reports use the email address of the person chatting with Echo.

Our team will reply by email for a verified bug or issue. Feedback may not receive a reply, but all feedback is read. This report route does not include ongoing customer support on Pro.
