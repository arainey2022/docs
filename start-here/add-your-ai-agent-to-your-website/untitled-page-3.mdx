# How to add My AskAI to HubSpot without Breeze

You can use My AskAI in HubSpot Service Hub without using Breeze as the support agent. My AskAI connects to HubSpot conversations and starts with private note replies.

## Important limit

The My AskAI integration works with HubSpot Service Hub conversations. It does not create HubSpot Ticketing System tickets.

## What you need

- A HubSpot Super Admin to install the app.
- A paid My AskAI plan.
- An authenticated HubSpot chat or email channel.
- A separate HubSpot account for the AI sender if you want a separate name and image.

The app requests `conversations.read` and `conversations.write`. It does not receive HubSpot Super Admin rights.

## Step 1: Connect HubSpot

In My AskAI, go to **Channels \> HubSpot** and select **Connect your HubSpot account**. Sign in, choose the correct HubSpot account and confirm.

## Step 2: Keep note replies on

By default, chat, social and email use note replies. Test a conversation and confirm that agents can see the draft.

<Frame>
  ![My AskAI note reply in HubSpot](https://mintcdn.com/myaskai/jQMqT5axxz3I9e3g/images/image-533.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=jQMqT5axxz3I9e3g&q=85&s=c0bf5bcc282950bb34b90c33d838e563)
</Frame>

## Step 3: Choose reply modes by channel

Go to **Dashboard \> Helpdesk & Channels \> HubSpot \> Type of Replies**. You can use note replies for email and direct replies for chat, or the reverse.

<Frame>
  ![Separate HubSpot reply modes by channel](https://mintcdn.com/myaskai/rqZPZCBQmxu6qsJ1/images/image-2.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=rqZPZCBQmxu6qsJ1&q=85&s=aacb0670b0dfb2c1d68b942897b33921)
</Frame>

## Step 4: Prepare direct chat replies

Before you turn on direct replies:

1. Disable Knowledge Base Search in competing HubSpot chatflows where it would answer first.
2. Set the chat as available 24/7 if that matches your support policy.
3. Remove a misleading typical reply time.

<Frame>
  ![Disable HubSpot knowledge-base search](https://mintcdn.com/myaskai/jQMqT5axxz3I9e3g/images/image-400.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=jQMqT5axxz3I9e3g&q=85&s=d54d686cd1b0e60562546ca1898337ad)
</Frame>

## Step 5: Turn on direct replies

In **Channels \> HubSpot**, turn on **Direct replies** for the selected channel. The AI account will assign the conversation to itself while it is handling it.

<Frame>
  ![HubSpot conversation assigned to AI account](https://mintcdn.com/myaskai/jQMqT5axxz3I9e3g/images/image-393.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=jQMqT5axxz3I9e3g&q=85&s=3926b76d02f46c98bf7cf8135d9286c2)
</Frame>

## Step 6: Test handoff

Ask to talk to a person. Confirm that the AI adds a handoff note, gives a summary, unassigns itself and stops replying.

<Frame>
  ![HubSpot human-handoff note](https://mintcdn.com/myaskai/jQMqT5axxz3I9e3g/images/image-534.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=jQMqT5axxz3I9e3g&q=85&s=c9be65c0e13a57681088a52725a8d081)
</Frame>

Once a HubSpot conversation is handed to a person, it cannot be passed back to the AI agent.

## Pause or roll back

- Add `Stop AI replies` as a note to stop one direct-reply conversation.
- Add `Stop writing AI notes` as a comment to stop note drafts. Do not send it as a customer reply.
- Pause all AI replies under **Channels \> HubSpot \> Pause AI agent**.
