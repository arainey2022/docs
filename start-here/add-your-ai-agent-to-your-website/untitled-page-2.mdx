# How to add My AskAI to Gorgias without Gorgias AI

You can keep Gorgias for email, tickets and chat while My AskAI supplies the AI replies. Install the My AskAI app, connect your Gorgias domain and start with internal notes.

## Before you start

- Use a paid My AskAI plan.
- Install My AskAI from the Gorgias App Store.
- Find the original Gorgias subdomain in your inbox URL.
- Connect knowledge to My AskAI.
- Decide which email and chat queues will take part in the test.

## Step 1: Install and connect

Install My AskAI in the Gorgias workspace. In My AskAI, go to **Channels \> Gorgias** and enter the Gorgias subdomain. Select **Connect your Gorgias account**.

## Step 2: Keep notes on for email and chat

Email and chat have separate reply settings. Keep both on **Internal Notes** for the first test.

<Frame>
  ![My AskAI internal note in Gorgias](https://mintcdn.com/myaskai/rqZPZCBQmxu6qsJ1/images/image-16.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=rqZPZCBQmxu6qsJ1&q=85&s=e320015637a5d16252ad57af5934908d)
</Frame>

## Step 3: Disable duplicate auto-replies

Turn off Gorgias email and chat auto-replies, including expected-wait-time messages, for the routes that My AskAI will handle.

<Frame>
  ![Gorgias auto-reply settings](https://mintcdn.com/myaskai/jQMqT5axxz3I9e3g/images/image-62.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=jQMqT5axxz3I9e3g&q=85&s=47cd102fc18f883fc6f86ef5f7fce812)
</Frame>

## Step 4: Test email and chat separately

Send one email ticket and one chat message. Confirm that each receives a note and gets the `ai-agent-replied` tag.

## Step 5: Test handoff

Ask to speak to a person. Confirm that Gorgias adds `human-handover-requested`, creates an internal note and stops further AI replies.

<Frame>
  ![Human handoff in Gorgias chat](https://mintcdn.com/myaskai/rqZPZCBQmxu6qsJ1/images/image-19.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=rqZPZCBQmxu6qsJ1&q=85&s=a67ec77c0da64596ff7b0a07e6d48175)
</Frame>

## Step 6: Turn on direct replies

Turn on direct replies for email or chat only after that channel passes testing. Select first-message-only for a low-risk email deployment.

## Important limit

My AskAI cannot currently use Gorgias triage steps or flows. Use email account, brand and reply-mode controls instead.

## Pause or roll back

- For a direct-reply conversation, reply as a person.
- For notes, add `Stop writing AI notes` as an internal note.
- Pause email and chat separately under **Channels \> Gorgias**.
- Disconnect the integration from the same connection page if required.
